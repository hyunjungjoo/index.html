Post-doctoral Researcher

Hanyang Institute for Phonetics and Cognitive Sciences of Language 222, Wangsimni-ro, Seoul, South Korea joohj0421@hanyang.ac.kr

I am a post-doctoral researcher at the Hanyang Institute for Phonetics and Cognitive Sciences of Language. I received my PhD in Linguistics from Rutgers University in 2026, with a dissertation entitled “The Representation and Computation of Intonation.”

My research takes computational, mathematical, and experimental approaches to fundamental questions in phonological theory — mathematically analyzing the complexity of prosodic structures and validating them with deep learning and experiments.
